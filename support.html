Server administrators need to configure the support role and ticket category environment variables for the bot service.

  • Ensure the bot can manage channels

03 / FAQ

Quick answers

Who can create a ticket?

Members who can view and use the posted ticket panel can request a ticket.

Who can close a ticket?

The ticket owner, an administrator, or a member with the configured support role can close it.

Why can’t the bot create a channel?

Check the bot's Manage Channels permission, the configured category ID, and the support role ID.

Where is the support server link?

The public invite link has not been provided yet. Add your official Discord invite in the website configuration before publishing.
